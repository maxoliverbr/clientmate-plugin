THE WORKFLOW

  1. /cmstrat — define who you should (and shouldn't) sell to
  2. /cmlist — build the tiered target account list
  3. /cmposer <account> — bad-fit check first. Drop if score < 40
  4. /cmmatch + /cmvalue <account> — map the deal, the champion, the real value
  5. /cmdevil — harden your pitch against a skeptical buyer
  6. /cmreach cmmatch-<account>.md — draft outreach to the champion
  7. /cmprep cmmatch-<account>.md — discovery call prep + disqualify triggers

8 COMMANDS

/cmstrat

Beachhead, Ideal Design Partner Profile, ICP, the asks — and the anti-patterns to refuse.

→ cmstrat.md
/cmlist

15–25 tiered candidate accounts ranked by pain, buyability, reference value and reachability.

→ cmlist.md
/cmposer

The core filter. Eight evidence-based checks, scored 0–100. A dream logo can still be a Walk Away.

→ cmposer-<account>.md
/cmmatch

7-dimension fit score, champion map, deal shape and a Pursue / Nurture / Pass verdict.

→ cmmatch-<account>.md
/cmvalue

Commercial + strategic value, netted against cost to serve. Your max justified concession.

→ cmvalue-<account>.md
/cmreach

Cold email, warm-intro request and LinkedIn DM built on the account's sourced, specific pain.

→ cmreach-<account>.md
/cmprep

Timed agenda, qualifying questions, objection handling and the triggers that tell you to walk.

→ cmprep-<account>.md
/cmdevil

A burned-before enterprise VP asks the 10 hardest deal-ending questions. Close the gaps.

→ cmdevil.md
